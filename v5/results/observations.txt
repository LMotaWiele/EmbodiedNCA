### Multi-seed + selection-log scrutiny (required for positive-looking Check 2)
Seeds {0,1,2,3,4} at dist 2 and 4 (same protocol). Policies are deterministic
(argmax / pure geometry), so all five seeds produce **identical** trajectories
(std=0). Result is therefore not a one-seed fluke of RNG:

| dist | selected | wear_aware | wellward | Δ(sel−wah) | seeds sel_better |
|-----:|---------:|-----------:|---------:|-----------:|-----------------:|
| 2 | 0.5120 | 0.4850 | 0.4850 | +0.0269 | 5/5 |
| 4 | 0.3855 | 0.3347 | 0.3456 | +0.0507 | 5/5 |

Selection-log (seed 0):
- dist=2: wear_aware boost sectors Counter({2:11, 3:1}) **≡** wellward;
  selected boosts more sparingly (7 vs 12) with heavy voluntary skip (140/151).
- dist=4: wear_aware ≈ wellward (both dominate sector 2 toward nearest/pre-degraded
  well A); selected diversifies (sectors 2,5,1,4) and still skips heavily.
- both-boost same-sector rate selected↔wear_aware: 4/7 (d2), 5/12 (d4).

### Honest reading of the Check 2 "win"
Absolute levels matter:
- selected on relocated ≈ selected on original (d2: 0.5120 vs 0.5124; d4: 0.3855 vs 0.3795)
- wear_aware **degraded** vs original (d2: 0.5188→0.4850; d4: 0.3915→0.3347)

So the relative win is real (selected beats the position-updated heuristic) but
**not** because the self-model suddenly improved — its absolute competence held
while the hand-coded wear rule lost angular discrimination: on this layout, the
fresher well (B at (28,4)) sits ~40° from the nearest well (A) from the pattern
placement, so 6-sector discretization collapses wear_aware into wellward (exact
match at d2). selected's frugal skip-heavy policy still edges both geometry
baselines.

Pattern classification remains **self_model_wins_or_ties_more**, with the caveat
that the margin change is driven more by heuristic collapse under sector
quantization than by a large transfer bonus for the model. Still a complete,
reportable outcome: once wells move, a one-line position update does not
automatically keep the heuristic at the original fair bar when geometry makes
"fresher well" and "nearest well" angularly close.

self_model_v3_5 does NOT support N≠2 wells without modification: fixed
n_wells=2 wear input channels. Per-well wear *mechanics* are variable-N, but
the frozen self-model's input encoding is two-slot. Concrete architectural
generalization limit — reported as the finding; no three-well run and no retrain.

## Project closing statement (v0–v5)

EmbodiedNCA (v0–v5) built a resource-gated embedded-pattern NCA world (v0), then
successively asked whether a learned self-model of future coherence / local
resource / survival could earn its complexity over hand-coded controls as
geometry (v2), asymmetric per-well wear (v3.5), chained identity (v4), and
finally **novel well layouts** (v5) were introduced. On fixed maps, correctly
parameterized heuristics matched or beat `selected`. On the closing zero-shot
relocation test, calibration **held** (no coordinate-memorization collapse for
prediction), `selected` **beat** position-updated heuristics with multi-seed
deterministic confirmation — largely because absolute model performance held
while the wear heuristic lost sector resolution when fresher- and nearest-well
directions nearly coincided — and variable well-count (N≠2) is architecturally
**unsupported** for the frozen self-model. The project's complete answer: this
stack produces a useful predictive self-model that transfers across two-well
layouts, but does not establish a general consciousness-adjacent controller
advantage; hand-coded geometry/wear rules remain competitive whenever their
parameters can be set, and fixed two-well input encoding is a hard stop for N>2.
Further work (continual learning / decision-driven plasticity) is a different
codebase and question, not Stage B of this one.
