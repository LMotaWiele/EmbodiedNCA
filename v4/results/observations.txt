### Check 4 interpretation
Segment-order reversal was retired (confounds order with entry wear).

**Control (wear-only):** front and back reset to identical fresh S/R/A with wear matched to floating-point noise. Under a deterministic simulator and frozen self-model, divergence is impossible by construction. A null here is a *sanity check that the apparatus has no hidden state or leakage* — not independent evidence that 'wear is a sufficient Markovian summary' as a scientific discovery. **Empirical residual-S/R probe:** this is where path dependence can actually appear. Divergence here is fully Markovian residual path dependence — history matters through state variables already tracked (S/R), not via a non-Markovian lifetime trace.
