## FINAL v2 VERDICT (read this first)

**Question v2 asked:** does self-model-driven action selection earn its
complexity under this design, on this geometry?

**Answer: no.**

Settled facts:

1. **Mean coherence:** selected does not raise mean coherence over random /
   round_robin / fixed / wellward_heuristic.
2. **Low-variance effect:** real, via sticky argmax (mode_frac≈0.96–0.98,
   switch_rate≈0.02). Switching baselines sawtooth; sticky policies are flat.
3. **6-way fixed sweep:** selected locks onto the map's structurally
   most-stable sector; fixed lock of that sector (no model) reproduces its
   variance. s0 (original single-draw fixed) was worst-in-sweep — beating it
   was a weak bar.
4. **wellward_heuristic (final control):**
   - **dist=4:** ≡ selected (mode=2, std 0.00084 vs 0.00095, mean_coh within
     0.001). Three-line geometric rule = full pipeline.
   - **dist=2:** modes differ. Selected → s4 (structural best, std=0.00031);
     wellward → s2 (geometric wellward, std=0.00078). Centroid ≈ on well
     (dist~0.24) so wellward angle is near-degenerate. Selected is slightly
     stabler than pure wellward but still ≡ fixed(s4) with no model.
5. **Naming:** not "confirms homeostasis" (v0 already did); not "reduce
   surprise" (objective is value-argmax). Additional allocation-stability
   on top of v0 base dynamics, fully matchable by zero-learning rules.

**v2 is done.** Do not add more candidates / horizons / selectors on this
single map. v3, if pursued, must face geometries a 3-line rule does not
trivially solve (competing wells, dynamic wells, nearer≠better) — which
requires retraining on varied geometries.
