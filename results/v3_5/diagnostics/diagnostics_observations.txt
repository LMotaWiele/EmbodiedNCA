- **verdict: NO_FLOOR_TIMING_SIGNAL**

### D2 interpretation
- Floor-crossing is cleanly decoupled from death (survives 3700 steps after
  first floor). There is **no skip-spike** at floor-crossing: voluntary skip
  rate is identically zero; boost rate is flat at the budget-limited ~2/3
  throughout.
- Stage A's dist=4 late-skip spike was **near-death, not floor-timing**.
- **Close the v3 timing hypothesis** as not supported by the self-model under
  this operating point. The model has the skip candidate and wear inputs, but
  does not use them for floor-aware restraint when the pattern is healthy.

## D3 — selected vs wear_aware: what differs + multi-seed

### Selection-log diff (dist=2, seed=0, Check 3 pre-degrade)
- sel mean_coh=0.5124  wah=0.5188  (Δ=−0.0064)
- alive boosts: selected=7  wear_aware=10
- common alive decisions: 15
  - both_act=7  only_sel=0  only_wah=3  both_skip=5
- when both act: **same_well=7 / diff_well=0**  same_sector=1 / diff_sector=6

Both always boost near well A (the pre-degraded well — geometry places the
pattern near A). wear_aware consistently picks sector 0 (angle toward the
fresher well B's direction from centroid, but nearest-well attribution of
the boost cell is still A because the pattern sits on A's side). selected
scatters across sectors 0–5 on the same well. The residual gap is **not about
which well** (they agree) and only weakly about sector identity; more of the
count difference is **only_wah=3** extra boosts by the heuristic.

### Multi-seed (placement angle varied; dynamics are deterministic at fixed center)
NOTE: seed alone does not diversify deterministic policies; we vary placement
angle (east / NE / SE) at fixed |distance|.

- dist=2: mean Δ(sel−wah)= **−0.0083 ± 0.0153**  [seed_sensitive_mixed]
  - seed0 ang=0.00: Δ=−0.0064
  - seed1 ang=+π/4: Δ=−0.0278
  - seed2 ang=−π/4: Δ=+0.0095  (selected wins this one, |Δ|<0.01)
- dist=4: mean Δ= **−0.0397 ± 0.0265**  [reproducibly_loses]
  - seed0: Δ=−0.0120
  - seed1: Δ=−0.0753
  - seed2: Δ=−0.0316
  - all three seeds: selected loses (sign-consistent; magnitude varies)

### D3 interpretation
- The Stage A "ties at dist=2 / loses at dist=4" ranking is **not pure noise**.
  Dist=4 is reproducibly a loss for selected across placement angles
  (mean Δ≈−0.04). Dist=2 is a genuine near-tie (mean Δ≈−0.008, within the
  MEANINGFUL_COH_DELTA=0.01 band; sign flips once at |Δ|<0.01).
- The residual is **not frequency of skipping alone** (counts are similar) and
  **not which well** (both always act on the pattern-side well under this
  pre-degrade geometry). It is a mix of (a) a few extra heuristic boosts
  (only_wah) and (b) sector choice within the same well — small absolute
  effect at dist=2, larger and consistent at dist=4.
- Treat "selected loses or ties wear_aware under asymmetric pre-wear" as a
  **reproducible finding**, not a single-seed fluke.

### D4 interpretation

- The 2 FN in val are statistical dust (2/282). Not worth architecture work.
- Do not block v4 planning on this; do not retrain for noise-death specifically
  unless the intervention or horizon changes so that noise becomes lethal.

## Bigger picture (before v4)

1. **local_R far-bin "collapse" is benign** (variance artifact). Near-bin local_R
   is usable. No evidence of a wear-asymmetry encoding failure for local_R.
2. **Floor-timing hypothesis is closed.** Decoupled D2: zero voluntary skips
   across floor-crossing with 3700 steps of post-floor life. Stage A dist=4
   skip spike was near-death.
3. **selected vs wear_aware is real and not single-seed noise.** Dist=4 loss
   reproduces across placements; dist=2 is a true near-tie. The gap is small
   and not explained by well choice — the heuristic still wins or ties.
4. **Noise-death "blind spot" is structural label rarity**, not a modeling gap.

This is now the second consecutive stage where a hand-coded rule matches or
beats a trained self-model (v2 geometry; v3.5 wear-aware). Items 1–3 above
are resolved cheaply and do **not** reveal a hidden capability the model has
that a bad encoding is masking. That removes the strongest "try the model
again with a better input" rationale for a v4 that adds yet another mechanism
hoping the self-model finally pulls ahead. Any next stage should either:
- target a regime where a single-comparison heuristic is *structurally*
  insufficient (true multi-step lookahead value), or
- accept the heuristic as the controller and use the self-model only where
  calibration is strong (coherence, survival, near-well local_R).
