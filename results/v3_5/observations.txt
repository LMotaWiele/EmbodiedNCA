### Central outcome (selected vs wear_aware_heuristic)
- dist=2: **ties** (|Δ|<0.01)
- dist=4: **loses_to** (Δ=−0.012)
- Honest summary: the learned self-model does **not** earn its complexity over
  the hand-coded wear-aware rule under asymmetric per-well wear at this OP.
  selected *does* beat the old geometry-only wellward_heuristic (the comparison
  that was design-foreclosed under global wear), but that is no longer the fair
  bar. wear_aware_heuristic — boost the fresher well, skip only when both
  floored — captures most of the available value with a single comparison.

### What selected seems to do
selected voluntary-skips heavily (140/151 and 131/151 decisions) after a few
early boosts. That is frugal relative to always_boost/wellward, and it beats
never_boost, but the hand-coded wear rule's slightly more targeted boosting
toward the less-worn well edges it out on mean coherence. Both selected and
wear_aware die early under the harsh pre-degrade (tod ~300–400); longevity is
not the differentiator here.

- Decisions counted only while pattern is alive (post-death skips are artifacts).

  - **verdict: INSUFFICIENT_DATA** for the post-floor recovery half of the
    prediction. Early bins show budget-limited boosting; late pre-death bins
    show rising voluntary skip (possibly near-death / low-R driven, not
    floor-timing).

- plot: results/check4_boost_timing.png

### Check 4 interpretation
The specific v3 timing prediction is **not confirmed**. At dist=2, where the
pattern lives past both floor-crossings, selected's boost rate is flat across
the floor boundary and fully explained by the attention budget — zero voluntary
skips. The skip option is available and competitive in the argmax, but the
trained model does not prefer it for floor-timing at this OP. Near-death skip
behavior at dist=4 is a different mechanism.

## Honesty notes
- A correctly implemented result that does not favor the learned model is a
  valid, reportable outcome. This stage produced one: wear_aware_heuristic ≥
  selected on the fair comparison.
- Unattributed ~−0.02 post-floor residual from v3 is not chased here.
- selected vs wellward under *global* wear was design-foreclosed; per-well wear
  + wear_aware_heuristic is the fair comparison, and the hand-coded rule wins
  or ties.
- Next lever (if desired): something that genuinely requires multi-step
  lookahead the heuristic cannot approximate with a single wear comparison —
  not assumed in advance, and not forced by cranking parameters.
